FAQ

Questions

Does my text leave my computer?

No. What you paste stays in Chrome's local storage on your machine. Auto Typer has no analytics and no server of its own. The only thing it ever sends anywhere is a Pro licence key, to our payment provider, to check it's valid. The privacy policy lists exactly what is stored and sent.

Why does Chrome say "Auto Typer started debugging this browser"?

Google Docs only accepts keystrokes that Chrome itself generates — it ignores anything a page or an ordinary extension simulates. The only way an extension can produce those real keystrokes is through Chrome's debugging interface, and Chrome shows that bar whenever it's in use. Auto Typer attaches only while it's typing and detaches when it finishes.

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes — it works the same in Chrome on Windows and macOS. It doesn't run on iPad or iPhone: Chrome on iOS doesn't support extensions.

Is there a free trial of Pro?

Yes — every Pro feature, free for 7 days. Start it from the extension: click any Pro feature, or open Settings → Account, and press "Try Pro free for 7 days". There's no card and no account. When the 7 days are up, Auto Typer goes back to the free plan by itself; nothing is charged and there's nothing to cancel. It's one trial per person.

What counts towards the free daily limit?

The characters of your text that actually get typed. Typos and their corrections don't count, and if you stop a run early, only what was typed counts. The limit resets at midnight.

I bought Pro — where's my key, and how do I switch it on?

The key is in your receipt email from Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. Paste it into Auto Typer under Settings → Account and press Activate. Step-by-step, with a picture — and what to do if you can't find the key.

Can I use Pro on more than one computer?

Yes, up to 3. To move it, open Settings → Account and choose "Deactivate on this device", then activate on the new one.
