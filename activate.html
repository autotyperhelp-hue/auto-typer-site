Switch Pro on in under a minute

Your licence key is in the receipt email from Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider.

  1. Copy the licence key from the receipt email — the whole thing, it looks like 38B1460A-5104-…
  2. Open Auto Typer: click its icon in Chrome's toolbar (if it isn't pinned, it's under the puzzle-piece icon), then open Settings → Account.
  3. Paste the key and press Activate. The badge at the top of the panel turns PRO, and multi-block and the other Pro settings unlock.

No Auto Typer yet? Add it to Chrome first — it's the same extension, the key just unlocks Pro.

Can't find the key?

Search your inbox — and spam — for the receipt. Or open My Orders on Lemon Squeezy and enter the email address you bought with: they send you a sign-in link, and the key is in the order. Still stuck? Email autotyper.help@gmail.com.

Using it on another computer

One key works on up to 3 of your own computers at a time. To move it, open Settings → Account on the old one and choose Deactivate on this device, then activate on the new one. If the old computer is gone, email us and we'll free the slot.
